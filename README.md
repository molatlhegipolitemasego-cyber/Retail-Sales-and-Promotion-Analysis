# Retail-Sales-and-Promotion-Analysis

**Project Overview**

This project uses Excel to analyse daily sales for a retail product. It examines selling prices, gross profit and how promotional pricing affects sales volumes, revenue and profitability.

**Objectives**
- Calculate daily selling prices and the average selling price per unit.
- Calculate gross profit, gross profit per unit and gross profit percentages.
- Estimate price elasticity of demand across three promotional periods.
- Present findings using calculations and charts.

**Tools Used**

Microsoft Excel for formulas, analysis and charts.

**Workbook Structure**
- Sales Master: Daily sales data and calculations.
- Promotion Analysis: Promotional comparisons and price elasticity calculations.
- Summary: Main results, monthly analysis and charts.
  
**Key Findings**

The average selling price was R35.40 per unit, and the product recorded an overall gross loss of R7.12 million. The three selected promotions increased sales volumes and revenue but generated gross losses.

*Recommendation**

Check promotional prices against unit costs and assess promotions using gross profit alongside revenue and sales volumes.
